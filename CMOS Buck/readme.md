In the subject of IC Concepts, my group implemented a continuous-time, closed-loop buck converter in 45nm CMOS technology. The circuit was fed by a battery power supply (3.3V-5V) and designed to step the voltage down to 1V. My main contribution was the control system, where I performed a non-traditional modeling of the plant and calculated the closed-loop gains for the state-feedback controller. Additionally, I worked on the hardware design, proposing the use of cascode MOSFETs to comply with the circuit's voltage limits. Although the project was not fully completed due to time constraints, the next steps were outlined, focusing on finding suitable driver topologies to handle the maximum voltage levels and implementing the control circuit within the hardware limitations.
